About

Meet Sarah

Sarah Griesemer is a psychologist, writer, and author examining how we absorb cultural rules around gender, anger, and caretaking. She began her career in child development before specializing in adult therapy and collaborative assessment, and she brings that developmental ear to her essays and fiction.

For years she led training groups for therapists on gender, maternal caretaking, and women's anger. Her writing looks at how women learn to manage and placate rather than risk confrontation.

She is the author of Self-Discovery Journal for First-Time Moms and the founder of The Book Shrink, where she reviews contemporary fiction through a psychologist's lens.

"I wrote my first children's book at age nine, and my grandmother helped me try to get it published. She took my words seriously before anyone else did. I've been writing ever since."
