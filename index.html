About the Author

A developmental ear for the stories we live inside.

Sarah is a psychologist, writer, and author examining how we internalize cultural rules around gender, anger, and caretaking. Beginning her career in child development before specializing in adult therapy and collaborative assessment, she brings a keen developmental ear to essays and fiction.

Her writing explores maternal domestic inequity and emotional management. Sarah is the author of Discovery Journal for First-Time Moms and the founder of The Book Shrink, where she reviews contemporary fiction through a clinical power lens.

For years she led therapist training groups on gender, maternal caretaking, and women's anger. Her writing looks at how women learn to manage and placate rather than risk confrontation, and asks who in our culture is granted permission to be furious.

"I wrote my first children's book at age nine, and my grandmother helped me try to get it published. She took my words seriously before anyone else did. I've been writing ever since, working through personal memoir, literary essays, and fiction to make sense of who we are."
